How it works

01

Photograph both

One photo or one short video for each of the two options you are choosing between.

02

The crowd hearts one

People tap the option they think is better, and leave advice explaining why.

03

You decide

Voting closes on your timer. You see the split, read the reasons, and make the call.

What Heartsay is not

Heartsay exists to settle a decision, not to judge people. It is not a dating app and not a rate-me app.

  • The subject of a post is the choice, never the person.
  • No nudity, no underwear or swimwear photos, nothing posed to show off a body.
  • No asking to be rated, and no requests for contact details.
  • There are no profiles to browse, no messaging between members, and no way to search for people — by design.
  • Every post and comment can be reported in one tap. A human decides within 24 hours.
  • Blocking someone removes their posts, advice and votes from your feed immediately.
